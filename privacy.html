Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

VILLALOBOS SB TRADING LLC operates the Villalobos Media TikTok Mini Application, a short drama and video streaming platform. This policy describes how we handle personal information in connection with the Mini Application.

1. Information we collect

We limit collection to the information needed to provide the features you use.

  • OpenID. TikTok Minis provides an app-specific user identifier through its login flow. We use it to maintain your player session and associate your viewing progress and favorites with your account.
  • Public nickname and avatar. If a feature needs to display your profile and you separately authorize access through TikTok, we may retrieve your public nickname and avatar. Declining profile access does not prevent the app from using OpenID for basic playback features.
  • Playback history and favorites. When you use these features, the app records your watched episodes, playback position and saved titles so you can resume viewing and find your favorites.

2. Video delivery and service providers

We use BytePlus VOD, a third-party video infrastructure provider, to store video assets and deliver streams with low latency. Video delivery may involve processing technical request information, such as an IP address and network or device information needed to serve and secure playback.

We share information with service providers only as needed to operate and support the service. We do not sell or rent personal data to any outside third party. We may disclose information where required by applicable law.

TikTok handles its platform login and authorization under its own privacy policies.

3. Retention and security

We retain account records and related data for as long as needed to provide the requested features, respond to support requests and meet applicable legal obligations. We limit access to account information to personnel and providers who need it to perform their work.

4. Delete your viewing data

You can request deletion of your Mini Application account history and associated metadata at any time by emailing aaes5616@outlook.com. This includes saved viewing history, playback progress, favorites and profile data we hold for your Mini Application account.

Include the app name and enough information for us to locate your record. We may ask for limited information to verify the request. Please keep passwords and verification codes out of your email. We will delete eligible information subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

5. Policy updates

We will post updates on this page and revise the effective date when this policy changes.
